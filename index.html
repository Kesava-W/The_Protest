Synopsis

Vivaswa notices everything.

The car that hit his father. The office politics that cost him his job. The routine of a stranger he has decided to destroy.

When a hit-and-run leaves his father fighting cancer and the driver resurfaces as the face of a staged protest, Vivaswa infiltrates the movement as a police informer — and quietly begins planning something the police were never meant to know about.

How far can a patient man go before the woman who loves him has to decide whether she still recognises him?

Main Characters

Vivaswa

A data entry operator with an unnervingly precise memory. Calm, observant, and willing to cross a line most people never approach.

Sandhya

Investigative journalist. Sharp, principled, and the only person Vivaswa never manages to hide anything from for long.

Vashista

Vivaswa's father. Gentle, unhurried, and quietly aware of far more than he ever asks about.

ACP Satvik

A police officer who trusts facts over instinct — and sees in Vivaswa an informer worth protecting, without ever learning the whole truth.
